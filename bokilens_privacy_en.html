Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

This app does not require its own account registration and does not transmit any personal information to our servers.

Your detailed learning records, journal entries, and drill-history storage data are stored only in the following locations:

  • On your device (the app's sandbox container)
  • iCloud Drive linked to your Apple ID (only if iCloud is enabled)

2. iCloud Sync

When iCloud is enabled, your learning data is automatically stored in iCloud Drive to sync across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID. Data is encrypted and managed by Apple's infrastructure; we cannot access it.

If you prefer not to use iCloud, turn off Boki Lens under iOS Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > iCloud Drive. In that case, data is stored only on your device.

3. In-App Purchases

Premium purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. This app does not collect or store your payment information.

4. Analytics & Advertising

To improve the app, we use TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH), a privacy-focused analytics service, to collect anonymous usage events. We send only an anonymized hashed identifier and interaction events that describe screens, features, learning-progress states, and purchase-success events.

We do not send the following information to TelemetryDeck:

  • Your journal entry contents, entered amounts, or free-form input
  • Your name, email address, or other contact information
  • Any advertising or tracking identifiers

Learning-progress events may include stage IDs, lesson IDs, solved counts, and correct counts. Common event context may include the display language (Japanese or English), your selected learning track (unselected, Japanese Bookkeeping, or Global Basics), and the content-experience version. We do not send your device region, country, or raw locale. Purchase-success events may include the plan name, product ID, and the category of the screen or feature where the purchase started. This category is limited to stage, drill, scenario, settings, or unknown; it does not include screen text, transaction IDs, prices, or Apple IDs. This app does not use advertising SDKs. Analytics data is not linked to you personally and is never used for advertising or tracking.

5. Deleting Your Data

  • Deleting the app from your device removes locally stored data.
  • Data in iCloud Drive remains. To delete it completely, go to iOS Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage > Boki Lens and choose "Delete Data."
