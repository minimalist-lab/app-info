SpeakFlip Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

minimalist-lab provides SpeakFlip (the "App"). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information Used by the App

1.1 Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information locally on your device and does not send it to our servers:

  • Learning progress, including attempts, correct counts, SRS intervals, and priority-card status
  • Custom vocabulary added by the user
  • Settings such as selected word set, practice focus, TTS speed, streak, and onboarding preferences
  • Notification permission state and reminder time
  • On-device analytics counts, such as screen and feature usage totals

1.2 Microphone and speech recognition

The speaking practice feature uses Apple's Speech Recognition framework. We do not send audio to our own servers and we do not store recordings. Depending on Apple's implementation and user settings, speech recognition may be processed by Apple servers.

1.3 Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple App Store / StoreKit. We do not receive or store credit card information.

2. Third Parties

We do not provide user information to third parties. The App does not include advertising networks, tracking SDKs, Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar third-party analytics services.

3. Export and Deletion

Users can export learning logs, analytics counts, and custom vocabulary as JSON files from the Word Manager screen. Users can also delete local learning data using the reset option in the App.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone: used to capture speech during speaking practice
  • Speech Recognition: used to judge spoken answers
  • Notifications: used only for optional daily reminders

6. Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact through-57-longer@icloud.com.

7. Changes

When this policy changes, the latest version will be posted on this page.
